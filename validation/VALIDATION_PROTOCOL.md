# Validation Protocol

## Validation Status

Founder use: extensively documented

External transfer: not yet established

Commercial validation: not established

Enterprise validation: not established

Current stage: controlled external pilot

## Purpose

Test whether P.Ai.O.S. can transfer beyond founder use with measured outcomes and limited founder involvement.

## Minimum Pilot Requirements

- One bounded use case.
- One participant or team.
- Baseline captured before intervention.
- Founder touchpoints logged.
- Outputs and outcomes captured.
- Limitations written before public claims are upgraded.
- Evidence grades assigned to every result.

## Required Artifacts

- `validation/BASELINE_FORM.md`
- `validation/INTERVENTION_LOG.md`
- `validation/FOUNDER_TOUCHPOINT_LOG.md`
- `validation/OUTCOME_CAPTURE.md`
- `validation/CASE_STUDY_TEMPLATE.md`

## Claim Boundary

A successful pilot may support a bounded transfer claim. It does not automatically prove broad transferability, commercial validation, enterprise validation, or causal effectiveness.
