# Evidence Ledger

This ledger tracks the public evidence classes that support P.Ai.O.S. claims. It is not a substitute for the private source archive.

| Evidence area | Public artifact | Evidence grade | What it supports | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Definition and scope | `README.md`, `START_HERE.md` | [P]/[V] | Public framing of the method and current status. | Framing does not prove transferability. |
| Operating loop | `docs/OPERATING_LOOP.md`, `examples/WORKED_EXAMPLE_001.md` | [V] | Shows the recurring method and a sanitized demonstration. | A demonstration is not an independent validation study. |
| Development sequence | `docs/OPERATIONAL_PHASES.md` | [V]/[R] | Founder archive supports the phase sequence. | External replication is not established. |
| Archive reconstruction | `reports/PAiOS_Final_Archive_Reconstruction_Report_v1.0.md` | [V] | Summarizes full-archive reconstruction and validation boundary. | Full source archive is private. Conversation-level evidence is not independent validation. |
| Cross-platform architecture history | `docs/ARCHITECTURE_HISTORY.md` | [V]/[AI]/[A]/[D] | Within the searched ChatGPT and Grok archives, supports the March 14, 2026 naming and formal structure, subsequent ChatGPT operationalization, and manually initiated observed cross-model handoffs. | Controlled sources remain private; platform coverage is incomplete; the record does not establish autonomous coordination, external transfer, causal effectiveness, commercial validation, or enterprise validation. |
| Marketing performance | `reports/PAiOS_Ecosystem_Performance_Marketing_Intelligence_Report_v1.3.md` | [V]/[V-OCR]/[D]/[A] | Supports the capture-bottleneck diagnosis and KPI baseline. | Platform metrics are date-bounded and must not be summed across units. |

| Founder case study | `case-studies/CASE_STUDY_000_FOUNDER_USE.md` | [V]/[A] | Bounded founder-use example. | Does not claim independent validation. |

## Evidence Labels

- [V] verified in archive or native export
- [R] user-reported
- [AI] AI interpretation
- [P] proposed
- [S] superseded
- [V-OCR] read from native dashboard screenshots and requiring live confirmation before external use
- [D] documented but not yet fully transcribed
- [A] operator-attributed context, not independently verified

AI ratings, summaries, or praise are not independent evidence.
