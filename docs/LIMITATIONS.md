# Limitations

This document states the current validation boundary plainly.

## Current Validation Status

Founder use: extensively documented

External transfer: not yet established

Commercial validation: not established

Enterprise validation: not established

Current stage: controlled external pilot

## What Remains Unproven

- Founder dependence remains.
- Causal effectiveness is not established.
- Broad transferability is not established.
- Commercial validation is not established.
- Enterprise validation is not established.
- AI assessments are not external validation.
- Cross-domain use does not establish subject-matter expertise in every domain.
- Public examples are selected and sanitized.

## What Public Evidence Can Show

Public artifacts can show:

- the method definition
- the operating loop
- founder-use reconstruction
- evidence discipline
- a worked example
- a validation protocol
- limitations and claims governance

Public artifacts do not prove that the method works for every operator, domain, organization, or outcome.
