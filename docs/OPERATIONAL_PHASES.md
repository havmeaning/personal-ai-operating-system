# P.Ai.O.S. Operational Phases

This document summarizes the development and operating phases of P.Ai.O.S. using evidence labels defined in the repository README. It is a public-facing reconstruction and intentionally avoids protected personal details.

| Phase | Period | Description | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prehistory | 2023-Jan 2026 | Personal knowledge, recovery, training, research, and decision problems existed before the named system. | [R]/[V] |
| Operating-system formation | Feb-Mar 2026 | Operating-system language, research architecture, evidence practices, cognitive infrastructure, and early P.Ai.O.S. concepts emerged. | [V] |
| Platform formation | Mar-Apr 2026 | Public platforms were created, branded, configured, and tested independently. Publishing infrastructure was still being assembled. | [V] |
| Ecosystem activation | Apr-Jun 2026 | Major platforms became active as a connected publishing and distribution system. Cross-platform content production, mobile execution, AI-assisted workflows, and reporting cadence accelerated. | [V] |
| Productization | May 2026 | Books, audiobooks, workbooks, bundles, pricing, compliance architecture, and governance applications were developed. | [V]/[R] |
| Evidence integration | Jun 2026 | YouTube history, BJJ evidence, dashboards, development records, analytics, and report archives were connected. | [V] |
| Evaluation and evidence freeze | Late Jun-Jul 2026 | Publishing cadence intentionally declined while the operator shifted from expansion to measurement, archive reconstruction, evidence validation, GitHub organization, documentation governance, and transfer design. | [V] |
| External validation | Jul 2026 onward | Pilot kits, validation protocols, case-study shells, transfer testing, and controlled external use. | [V]/[P] |

The decline in publishing cadence after late June 2026 was intentional. It should not be interpreted as abandonment, loss of momentum, or reduced capability. It marked a shift from platform formation and expansion into evaluation, evidence consolidation, and transfer design.

## Validation Boundary

The phase model supports a documented founder-use and development-history claim. It does not establish generalized effectiveness, independent transfer, enterprise validation, commercial validation, or guaranteed outcomes.
