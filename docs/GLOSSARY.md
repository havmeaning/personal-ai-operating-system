# Glossary

## P.Ai.O.S.

Personal AI Operating System. A documented operating method for turning fragmented information and lived activity into structured decisions, governed evidence, executable actions, and reusable reports.

## Operating Loop

Capture -> Structure -> Analyze -> Decide -> Execute -> Report -> Extract Doctrine.

## Doctrine

A reusable rule, template, checklist, or operating principle extracted from a completed operation.

## Evidence Grade

A label that describes the strength and source of a claim.

## Founder Use

Use of the system by its creator/operator. Founder use can demonstrate development history and internal consistency, but it does not prove independent transfer.

## External Transfer

Use of the method by another operator with limited founder involvement and measured outcomes.

## Capture

The process of turning raw inputs into records that can be structured, reviewed, and used.

## Report Layer

The feedback system that turns action into review, correction, and doctrine.

## Public-Safe Summary

A derivative artifact that preserves the evidence claim while excluding protected source material.
