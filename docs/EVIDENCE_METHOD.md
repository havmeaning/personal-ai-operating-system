# Evidence Method

P.Ai.O.S. separates claims from records. A public claim should identify its evidence grade, source artifact, limitation, and review date.

## Claim Discipline

Every public claim should answer:

- What exactly is being claimed?
- What artifact supports it?
- What evidence grade applies?
- What does the evidence not prove?
- When was the claim last reviewed?
- Who owns the review?

The public claims register is the canonical index:

- `evidence/PUBLIC_CLAIMS_REGISTER.csv`

## Evidence Grades

- [V] verified in archive or native export
- [R] user-reported
- [AI] AI interpretation
- [P] proposed
- [S] superseded
- [V-OCR] read from native dashboard screenshots and requiring live confirmation before external use
- [D] documented but not yet fully transcribed
- [A] operator-attributed context, not independently verified

## Rules

1. Do not convert repeated language into fact.
2. Do not treat AI praise as validation.
3. Do not sum unlike metrics across platforms.
4. Do not publish protected source records merely to prove they exist.
5. Do not upgrade a claim without updating the claims register.
6. State limitations next to claims, not in a separate apology section.
